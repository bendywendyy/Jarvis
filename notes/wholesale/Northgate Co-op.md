# Northgate Co-op

Member-owned grocery co-op on the north side of Port Alder, with about 4,000 members. They stock our 12 oz retail bags in the coffee aisle.

## Account
- About **40 bags/week** across 3 coffees: [[Harbor Dark]], the Colombia single origin and a decaf.
- They buy at $11.20/bag from 1 October (see [[Wholesale Pricing]]) and sell at $14.99–$15.49.
- Buyer: Marcus Oyelaran. Orders every Wednesday for Friday delivery.
- Sales up 22% since we got an end-cap display in June 2026.

## Asks from Northgate
- **[[compostable packaging]] by January 2027.** It's a co-op-wide policy for private and local brands. This needs the compostable bag from [[Bayside Packaging]] (+$0.29/bag) and an order by early November.
- Monthly in-store tasting demos. [[Tom Whitaker]] did two in the summer and each lifted that week's sales by about 30%.
- Roast date printed clearly on every bag (we already do this).

## Considerations
- If compostable bags go co-op only, we'd run two bag SKUs. It's simpler to switch everything, and that fits the [[Brand Guidelines]] sustainability story.
- The county inspector flagged that a permit may be needed if retail outlets grow past 10 (see [[Health Inspection 2026]]).
