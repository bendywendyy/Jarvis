# Seaview Hotel

Our **largest wholesale account**: a 120-room hotel on the Esplanade with a restaurant, lobby café and room service. Contact: Laura Kim, F&B Director.

## Account facts
- About **55 lb/week**: [[Harbor Dark]] for espresso, plus a decaf.
- Roughly $28k/year in revenue at the old prices, which is about 15% of wholesale revenue.
- On the >50 lb tier. They go from $9.90 to $10.50/lb on 1 October (see [[Wholesale Pricing]]).
- We loan them a two-group espresso machine and service it quarterly (see [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]]).

## Contract renewal: due 15 November 2026
- The current 2-year contract expires 15 November.
- Laura has had an approach from a national roaster offering $9.20/lb plus free equipment.
- Our pitch: a **custom "Seaview Signature" blend** (+$0.75/lb custom fee waived for a 2-year term), a staff latte art training day, and faster service turnaround.
- [[Tom Whitaker]] and [[Maya Okafor]] are presenting the proposal on **21 October**.
- Walk-away point: we won't go below $10.10/lb, because margin gets too thin with green costs up 18%.

## Risks
- Losing Seaview would cut wholesale volume by about 16% and leave the roaster even further under capacity (see [[Roasting Schedule]]).
- It's on the list of [[Q4 priorities]].
