# Driftwood Bakery

Bakery two streets over on Cannery Row. It's both a **wholesale customer** (they serve our coffee) and a **supplier** (they bake our pastries). Owner: Sam Delgado.

## As a customer
- About **18 lb/week** of [[Harbor Dark]], moving to the $12.25 tier on 1 October (see [[Wholesale Pricing]]).
- Account since 2020, one of our first wholesale customers.
- **Problem: late payment.** Average days-to-pay in 2026 is **41 days** on net-30 terms. The worst was 67 days in May. They currently owe $1,480, with $610 more than 30 days overdue.

## As a supplier
- Daily pastry delivery at 6:30am (croissants, morning buns, scones). About $1,900/month.
- Good quality, and customers love the morning buns. The pastries were fine at the [[Health Inspection 2026]].

## Options discussed
1. Offset: net their coffee invoice against our pastry invoice each month. Sam likes this idea.
2. Move them to net-15 or prepay.
3. Leave it as is. Not great given cash is tight (see [[Cash Flow Q3 2026]]).

Decision at the [[Team Meeting 2026-09-08]]: try the **monthly offset** from October. [[Tom Whitaker]] will set it up with the bookkeeper, and Maya will tell Sam directly because they're friends.
