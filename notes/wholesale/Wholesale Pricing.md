# Wholesale Pricing

Price list for café, restaurant and office accounts. New tiers take effect **1 October 2026**, following the [[Pricing Review]].

## Tiers (per lb, roasted, 5 lb bags)
| Weekly volume | Old price | New price (from 1 Oct) |
|---|---|---|
| Under 20 lb | $11.50 | $12.25 |
| 20–50 lb | $10.75 | $11.40 |
| Over 50 lb | $9.90 | $10.50 |

Single origins are +$1.50/lb on every tier. A custom blend is +$0.75/lb, with a 50 lb/week minimum.

## Terms
- **Net-30** for accounts older than 6 months. Prepay for the first 3 orders.
- Delivery Tuesday and Friday within 25 miles. Free over 20 lb, otherwise $12.
- Free barista training session (2 hours) at onboarding. Equipment loan available for accounts over 40 lb/week, with a servicing agreement.
- Late payment: 1.5% per month after 45 days. We have never actually enforced this, and that needs to change (see [[Driftwood Bakery]]).

## Retail accounts
Grocery shelf accounts like [[Northgate Co-op]] buy 12 oz retail bags at $11.20 each (new price), and the retailer marks them up about 35%.

## Margin check
At the new prices, gross margin on Harbor Dark wholesale is about 46% (it fell to 41% at the old prices once green costs rose). Target is 45%+.
