# Instagram Strategy

Instagram is our main marketing channel. **6,200 followers** as of late September 2026, and the goal is **8,000 by 31 December 2026**.

## Cadence
- 3 feed posts or reels per week (Mon, Wed, Sat) plus stories most days.
- Best time to post: **7–8am**, when people are deciding where to get coffee.
- Boosting budget is $150/month, spent only on posts that are already doing well organically.

## What works (from Q2–Q3 data)
1. **Roasting reels**. Beans dropping into the cooling tray get about 3× the reach of anything else. Film on Wednesday wholesale roasts (see [[Roasting Schedule]]).
2. Staff faces and latte art (with consent, per the [[Staff Handbook]]).
3. Honest posts. The pricing explainer post had our highest comment count ever (see [[Pricing Review]]).
4. Origin stories: farm photos from [[Finca La Esperanza]]. The February 2027 trip should give a month of content.

## What doesn't
- Polished product shots on white backgrounds.
- Generic "happy Monday" posts.

## Campaigns coming up

- A [[Loyalty Program]] push: "double stamps week" in late October to bring back lapsed members.
- A holiday gift [[Subscription Service]] campaign from mid-November.

## Tone & visuals
Follow the [[Brand Guidelines]]: natural light, Harbor Navy and Rope Tan, and no stock photos. Captions should sound like a person wrote them.
