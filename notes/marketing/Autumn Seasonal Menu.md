## Food
- Pear & almond galette from [[Driftwood Bakery]], $5.25. Sam is testing it the week of 29 September.

## Launch plan
- Syrup batch testing: 29 September to 1 October. We make the syrups in-house, with a 5-day shelf life, so they go on the daily prep list.
- Staff training: 2–3 October. Every barista makes each drink twice.
- Instagram teaser: 1 October, and launch reel: 6 October (see [[Instagram Strategy]]).
- Email to [[Loyalty Program]] members on launch morning.
- Order extra syrups and chestnut purée as part of the weekly [[Inventory Process]].

## Targets
- 12% of drink sales from seasonal drinks (last autumn: 9%).
- Keep drink ticket times under 4 minutes at the morning peak, because the mocha is slow. Consider batching the chestnut sauce.
