# Brand Guidelines

Short version of the brand book (v2.1, refreshed in January 2026).

## Name & logo
- Always "Harbor Lane Coffee Roasters" in full on first mention, then "Harbor Lane". Never "HLC".
- The logo is the rope-knot mark plus the wordmark. Keep clear space equal to the knot's height. Don't recolor it or put it on busy photos.

## Colors
| Name | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Harbor Navy | #1F3A5F | Primary. Bags, signage, headers |
| Rope Tan | #C8A97E | Accents, kraft bag labels |
| Sea Glass | #A8D5C2 | Seasonal and limited releases only |
| Sail White | #F7F4EE | Backgrounds |

## Type
- Headlines: a slab serif, bold, all caps for bag names.
- Body: a clean humanist sans.

## Voice
Warm, plainspoken, a little salty. We sound like a neighbour who knows a lot about coffee, not a sommelier. Short sentences. Be honest about costs and prices (see how we explained the [[Pricing Review]]). No "artisanal", "curated" or "elevated".

## Sustainability claims
- Only claim what we can prove. Say "industrially compostable" and not "compostable" or "eco-friendly" on its own.
- When we switch to [[compostable packaging]] from [[Bayside Packaging]], the bag must say where it can be composted.
- For [[Finca La Esperanza]], publish the price paid per lb.

## Photography
Natural light, real staff, real customers (with permission, per the [[Staff Handbook]]). The roaster in action is our best content (see [[Instagram Strategy]]).
