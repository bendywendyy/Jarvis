# Loyalty Program

A digital stamp card on the POS, launched **4 May 2026** to replace the paper punch cards. Set up by [[Priya Nair]].

## How it works
- Customers sign up at the till with a phone number or email.
- **Buy 9 drinks, get the 10th free**. Any drink, any size.
- A free birthday drink if they add their birthday.
- Stamps expire after 6 months of inactivity.

## Numbers (to 25 September 2026)
- **1,140 members**, about 55 new sign-ups per week at the start and about 25/week now.
- **22% redemption rate**: of members who reached 9 stamps, 22% have redeemed.
- Members visit **2.1× per week** on average versus an estimated 1.3× for non-members.
- Cost: about **$1,350/month** in free drinks at retail value, which is roughly $420/month at cost. It's covered by the marketing line in the [[2026 Budget]].

## Watch items
- After the price increase on 1 October (see [[Pricing Review]]), track member visit frequency weekly. A drop below 1.8×/week for 3 weeks means we should rethink.
- About 30% of members haven't visited in 45+ days. Plan a "double stamps week" win-back in late October (see [[Instagram Strategy]]).

## Ideas under discussion
- Tiers ("Deckhand" / "Captain"). Captain members would get 10% off retail bags.
- Linking the program to the [[Subscription Service]], for example a free drink with every subscription renewal.
- Using the email list for the [[Autumn Seasonal Menu]] launch.
