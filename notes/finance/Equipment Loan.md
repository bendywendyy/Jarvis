# Equipment Loan

Term loan from **Port Alder Credit Union** used to buy the 15 kg drum roaster and afterburner in March 2024.

## Terms
- Principal: **$85,000**
- Rate: **7.2% fixed**
- Term: 60 months (March 2024 to February 2029)
- Payment: **$1,690/month**, auto-debited on the 3rd
- Balance at the end of September 2026: about **$46,300**
- Secured on the roaster itself, plus a personal guarantee from [[Maya Okafor]]

## Covenants
- Debt service coverage ratio of at least **1.25×**, tested annually on the December year-end accounts. It was 1.62× for 2025.
- Quarterly management accounts go to the credit union within 45 days of quarter-end. Q3 is due by 14 November.

## What it bought
- 15 kg roaster (~$68,000 installed). It replaced the old 5 kg machine and made wholesale growth possible (see [[Roasting Schedule]]). We're currently at about 55% of its capacity.
- Afterburner and ducting (~$14,000). This is required by the county for the roastery permit.
- Installation and electrical work (~$3,000).

## Options being considered
- Refinancing: the credit union has hinted at 6.4% if we move the business checking account over. That saves about $950 over the remaining term. Probably not worth the hassle.
- Extra principal payments in December if the holiday season is strong. There's no prepayment penalty.

The $25,000 line of credit mentioned in [[Cash Flow Q3 2026]] is a separate facility with the same lender. See also [[2026 Budget]].
