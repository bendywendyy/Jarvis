# 2026 Budget

Approved by [[Maya Okafor]] on 15 December 2025. Reviewed quarterly (see [[Quarterly Review Q2 2026]]).

## Revenue target: $1,050,000
| Stream | Target | Share |
|---|---|---|
| Café (drinks, food, retail bags in store) | $740,000 | 70% |
| Wholesale | $190,000 | 18% |
| Online store & [[Subscription Service]] | $120,000 | 12% |

## Cost targets
- **COGS: 34%** of revenue. Green coffee, milk, pastries, packaging.
- **Labor: 31%**, including payroll taxes. 11 staff (4 FT baristas, 3 PT, head roaster, café manager, wholesale lead, owner draw budgeted separately).
- Rent: $4,800/month for café and roastery (lease runs to 2029).
- [[Equipment Loan]] repayments: $1,690/month.
- Marketing: **3% of revenue** (~$31,500). Covers [[Instagram Strategy]] boosting, the [[Loyalty Program]] (the free drinks count here) and events.
- Repairs & maintenance: $9,000, which includes quarterly espresso servicing.
- Target net profit: **7%** (~$73,500).

## Assumptions that have already moved
- Green coffee was budgeted at +8% but is running at **+18%** (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]).
- Milk was budgeted flat but is up 9%.
- These are why COGS ran at 36.5% in Q2 and why we did the [[Pricing Review]].

## Not in the 2026 budget
- The Colombia origin trip (February 2027, ~$4,800).
- POS-linked inventory software.
- Compostable bags for everything (+$3,800/year). This will need a decision for the 2027 budget.
