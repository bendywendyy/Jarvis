# Pricing Review

Decision made 18 August 2026 after Q2 margins came in 2.5 points under budget (see [[Quarterly Review Q2 2026]]). Takes effect **1 October 2026**.

## Why
- Green coffee is up 18% year-on-year (see [[Green Coffee Sourcing]]).
- Milk is up 9%, and oat milk is up 6%.
- The state minimum wage rises in January 2027, and our starting pay needs to stay ahead of it (see [[Staff Handbook]]).
- We haven't raised café prices since March 2024.

## Changes
| Item | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| Latte / flat white (12 oz) | $5.25 | $5.50 |
| Drip coffee (12 oz) | $3.50 | $3.75 |
| Espresso | $3.25 | $3.25 (unchanged, it's our "anchor" price) |
| Oat milk upcharge | $0.75 | $0.75 (unchanged) |
| 12 oz retail bag, [[Harbor Dark]] | $18.00 | $19.50 |
| 12 oz single origin | $21.00 | $22.50 |
| Wholesale | see [[Wholesale Pricing]] | about +6% across tiers |

- **Subscriptions:** existing subscribers keep their current price until 1 January 2027, then +$1.00 per shipment. New subscribers pay the new price immediately (see [[Subscription Service]]).

## Expected impact
- About **+$36,000/year** in gross profit if volume holds.
- We assume a 2–3% drop in café transactions for about 6 weeks. We'll watch [[Loyalty Program]] visit frequency as an early warning.

## Communication
- A small counter sign from 24 September: "Coffee costs are up, so our prices are too. Here's why." A short, honest explanation in our voice per the [[Brand Guidelines]].
- Wholesale accounts got letters from [[Tom Whitaker]] on 1 September (30 days' notice).
