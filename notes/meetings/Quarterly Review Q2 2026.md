# Quarterly Review Q2 2026

Held 14 July 2026 by [[Maya Okafor]] with the three leads. The numbers come from the bookkeeper's June close.

## Revenue: $271,300 vs $262,500 target (+3.4%)
| Stream | Actual | Target | Comment |
|---|---|---|---|
| Café | $196,800 | $185,000 | +6.4%. Strong spring, and the [[Loyalty Program]] launched in May |
| Wholesale | $45,300 | $47,500 | −4.6%. No new accounts in Q2 |
| Online & subscriptions | $29,200 | $30,000 | −2.7%. Subscriber count falling |

## Margins
- **COGS at 36.5% vs 34% budget.** Green coffee is up 18% and milk is up 9%, and this is the reason for the [[Pricing Review]].
- Labor at 30.2%, which is under budget but only because we were short a barista for 3 weeks.
- Net profit for the quarter is about 5.1% versus the 7% target.

## What went well
- Café growth and the loyalty program take-up (620 members in the first 8 weeks).
- Health inspection score of 96 (see [[Health Inspection 2026]]).
- Northgate end-cap display lifted retail bag sales by 22%.

## What didn't
- Wholesale is behind [[Tom Whitaker]]'s target. Prospecting time is being eaten by deliveries.
- [[Subscription Service]] churn is at 6.8% per month.
- The oat milk stock-outs.

## Decisions
1. Run a pricing review in August. This happened, and the new prices apply from 1 October.
2. Tom gets every Tuesday afternoon blocked for prospecting. The Friday delivery run moves to a part-time driver in Q4 if budget allows.
3. Build a "pause subscription" feature before the holidays.
4. Revisit the [[2026 Budget]] assumptions in the Q3 review in October.
