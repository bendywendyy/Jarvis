# Roasting Schedule

Roasting happens **Monday, Wednesday and Friday** on the 15 kg drum roaster. We average **22 batches a week** (about 290 kg roasted). [[Daniel Reyes]] owns the schedule and posts it every Friday afternoon.

## Weekly rhythm
- **Monday**: café stock, mostly [[Harbor Dark]] for espresso plus whichever single origin is on filter. Espresso rests 48 hours before use (degassing), so Monday roasts go on the bar from Wednesday.
- **Wednesday**: wholesale orders for Thursday packing and Friday delivery. [[Seaview Hotel]] alone is about 4 batches.
- **Friday**: online orders and the [[Subscription Service]] (packed Friday afternoon, shipped Monday morning), plus [[Northgate Co-op]] retail bags.

## Rules of thumb
- Batch size is 12 kg green for Harbor Dark and 10 kg for single origins (lighter roasts need more airflow).
- Roast loss is 15–17% for Harbor Dark and 13–14% for light single origins.
- The first batch of the day is always a warm-up batch of Harbor Dark.
- Log every batch (charge temp, first crack time, development %, drop temp) in the roast log.

## Capacity
The roaster could do about 40 batches a week, so we are at roughly 55% of capacity. Holiday season (Nov–Dec) pushes us to about 30 batches, and we add a Tuesday session.
