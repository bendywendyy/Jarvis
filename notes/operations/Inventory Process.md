# Inventory Process

We do a full count **every Sunday at close**. Counts go into the shared inventory spreadsheet ("Stock 2026") before Monday 9am so orders can be placed.

## Café stock (owner: [[Priya Nair]])
| Item | Par level | Supplier / notes |
|---|---|---|
| Whole milk | 24 gallons | Dairy delivery Mon/Thu |
| Oat milk | 18 cartons (case of 6) | Oat milk now 31% of milk drinks |
| 12 oz hot cups | 2 sleeves of 1,000 | |
| Pastries | Daily order | [[Driftwood Bakery]], ordered by 2pm the day before |
| Syrups | 2 bottles each | Seasonal syrups for the [[Autumn Seasonal Menu]] |

## Roastery stock (owner: [[Daniel Reyes]])
- Green coffee: keep at least **6 weeks of cover** for every coffee in the [[Single Origin Lineup]] and Harbor Dark components. Reorder from the importer when cover falls below 8 weeks, because lead time is 3–4 weeks.
- Retail bags and labels from [[Bayside Packaging]]: reorder at 2,000 bags, since lead time is 6 weeks and the minimum order is 5,000.
- Degassing valves and mailer boxes for the [[Subscription Service]].

## Known issues
- Oat milk ran out twice in August. The par was raised from 12 to 18 cartons.
- Spreadsheet formulas broke when someone sorted the sheet. The columns are now locked.
- We would like POS-linked inventory eventually, but it's too expensive for the 2026 budget.

The weekly count takes about 45 minutes with two people.
