# Health Inspection 2026

County health inspection on **12 March 2026**. Unannounced, around 10:30am on a Thursday. [[Priya Nair]] was on shift and walked the inspector through.

## Result
**96 / 100. Passed.** Up from 93 in 2025.

## Findings (−4 points)
1. **Hand sink partially blocked** by the mop bucket (−2). Corrected on the spot. The closing checklist now says to keep the mop bucket away from the sink.
2. **No thermometer in the under-counter milk fridge** (−1). A thermometer was bought the same day, and temperatures are now logged twice daily (see [[Opening and Closing Checklist]]).
3. **One staff member without a current food handler card** (−1). Completed within the week. The [[Staff Handbook]] now requires it within 30 days of starting.

## Positives noted
- Good separation of roastery and café areas.
- Pastry storage and labelling from [[Driftwood Bakery]] were fine.
- Cleaning logs for the espresso machine were up to date.

## Follow-up
- Next routine inspection expected around March 2027.
- The inspector mentioned the roastery may need a separate permit if we start selling roasted coffee to more than 10 retail outlets. Worth checking before we expand [[Northgate Co-op]]-style accounts.
