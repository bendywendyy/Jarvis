# Opening and Closing Checklist

Laminated copy lives by the till. The shift lead initials each section on the daily sheet.

## Opening (6:15am, doors at 7:00am)
1. Disarm alarm, lights, music (morning playlist).
2. Turn on espresso machine if it was powered down, and check boiler pressure.
3. Dial in espresso: Harbor Dark at 18 g in, 38 g out, 27–31 seconds. Taste before service.
4. Brew first batch of filter (current single origin).
5. Receive pastry delivery from [[Driftwood Bakery]] (6:30am) and check against the order.
6. Record fridge temperatures on the log sheet. Must be ≤ 41°F. This is required since the [[Health Inspection 2026]].
7. Float the till with $200.
8. Wipe tables and put out the A-frame sign.

## Closing (doors lock 4:00pm weekdays, 3:00pm Sundays)
1. Backflush and clean the machine per [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]].
2. Clean the grinders, empty the hoppers of single origin (Harbor Dark can stay).
3. Mop the floor. **Keep the mop bucket away from the hand sink.**
4. Record fridge temperatures again.
5. Cash-up and count the till. Drop the safe bag. Variances over $5 go in the log.
6. Take out the trash and recycling. Coffee grounds go in the compost bin for the community garden.
7. Sunday only: full stock count per the [[Inventory Process]].
8. Set alarm and lock both doors.
