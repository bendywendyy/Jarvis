# Staff Handbook

Summary of the handbook every new starter signs. Full PDF is in the shared drive.

## Pay & tips
- Starting barista pay is **$17.50/hour**, rising to $18.75 after passing Level 2 certification.
- Tips are pooled weekly and split by hours worked. Card tips are paid out with payroll every other Friday.
- Roastery staff are paid $21/hour and are not in the café tip pool.

## Time off & scheduling
- Request time off at least **2 weeks** in advance through the rota app. Priya approves café requests and Daniel approves roastery ones.
- Shift swaps are fine if the manager on duty is told in the group chat.
- Late arrival more than 3 times in a month means a conversation with [[Priya Nair]].

## Training
- New baristas shadow **3 shifts** before working the bar alone.
- Level 1: espresso dial-in, milk steaming, [[Opening and Closing Checklist]].
- Level 2: latte art, [[Espresso Machine Maintenance]] (daily and weekly tasks), cash-up.
- Level 3 (optional): cupping with [[Daniel Reyes]] and roastery basics.

## Perks & policies
- Free drinks on shift. One free 12 oz bag of coffee per month.
- 30% staff discount on retail bags and merchandise.
- Food safety: everyone completes the online food handler course within 30 days. This came up at the [[Health Inspection 2026]].
- Social media: staff may post about the café, but follow the [[Brand Guidelines]] tone and never post customer faces without permission.
