# Espresso Machine Maintenance

Our two-group espresso machine does about 450 shots on a busy Saturday. Downtime costs roughly $900 per morning in lost sales, so maintenance is not optional.

## Daily (closing barista)
- Backflush each group with detergent. Water-only flush at midday.
- Soak portafilters and baskets in cleaner overnight.
- Purge and wipe steam wands after every drink. Soak the wand tips at close.
- Empty and wipe the drip tray.

## Weekly (Sunday close)
- Check group gaskets and shower screens. Replace screens if the water spray is uneven.
- Clean the grinder burrs with grinder cleaning tablets.
- Record water hardness from the filter test strip.
