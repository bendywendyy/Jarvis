# Finca La Esperanza

Family farm in **Pitalito, Huila, Colombia**, run by Luis Cárdenas and his daughter Valentina. 14 hectares at 1,650–1,800 m. Castillo and Caturra varieties, washed process.

## Relationship
- Third harvest buying directly (2024, 2025, 2026). We were introduced by an importer in 2023 and then moved to direct trade.
- 2026 contract: **60 bags (70 kg each), 4,200 kg total**, at **$4.60/lb FOB**. Up from $4.10/lb in 2025.
- Payment terms: 40% on signing (paid in August 2026, $17,040), with the balance ($25,550) on arrival in November.
- Shipping and import are handled through Pacific Green Importers for a flat fee of $0.38/lb.

## Quality
- Cupping scores: 85.5 (2024), 86.0 (2025), 85.75 (2026 pre-shipment sample).
- Tasting notes: red apple, panela, milk chocolate.
- It's the backbone of [[Harbor Dark]] (60% of the blend) and is sold on its own as part of the [[Single Origin Lineup]].
