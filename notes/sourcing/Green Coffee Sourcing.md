# Green Coffee Sourcing

How we buy green (unroasted) coffee. [[Daniel Reyes]] evaluates, and [[Maya Okafor]] signs contracts.

## Principles
- Only buy lots that cup at **84+** on our Thursday cupping table.
- Prefer long-term relationships over chasing auction lots. Our goal is 50% of volume through direct or relationship trade by 2027. We're at about 35% today.
- Pay at least 25% above the commodity "C" price for every lot, and publish what we paid for relationship coffees.

## Where our coffee comes from (2026)
| Origin | Share of volume | Route |
|---|---|---|
| Colombia, Huila | ~35% | Direct: [[Finca La Esperanza]] |
| Brazil, Cerrado Mineiro | ~30% | Importer: Pacific Green Importers |
| Ethiopia, Guji | ~15% | Importer: Pacific Green Importers |
| Guatemala, Huehuetenango | ~12% | Importer (spot) |
| Other / limited | ~8% | Sample lots |

## Costs
- The average landed cost of green coffee is up **18% year-on-year** ($4.05/lb in 2025 → $4.78/lb in 2026). This is the main driver behind the [[Pricing Review]].
- Colombia is prepaid 40% at harvest contract signing. That's the $17,040 payment that squeezed August (see [[Cash Flow Q3 2026]]).

## Process
1. Request samples (200 g) from importer offer sheets.
2. Sample roast on Tuesday afternoon, then cup on Thursday.
3. Approve, then Maya signs a forward contract, usually 3–6 months out.
4. Update the six-weeks-of-cover plan in the [[Inventory Process]].

Components feed [[Harbor Dark]] and the [[Single Origin Lineup]].
