# Harbor Dark

Our house blend and bestseller. It's the espresso in every café milk drink, and it accounts for **38% of all roasted volume**.

## Components
- **60% Colombia, [[Finca La Esperanza]]**: body, sweetness, red fruit.
- **40% Brazil, Cerrado Mineiro** (via Pacific Green Importers): chocolate, nuttiness, low acidity.
- Recipe changed in 2024. It used to be 50/30/20 with a Sumatra component, which we dropped for consistency.

## Roast profile
- Medium-dark. Drop at 224°C, development time ratio about 22%, total time 12:30–13:00.
- 12 kg green batches, with 15–17% roast loss (see [[Roasting Schedule]]).
- Rest 48 hours minimum before espresso use.

## Tasting notes
Dark chocolate, toasted almond, molasses, with a clean finish.

## Espresso recipe (café)
18 g in → 38 g out, 27–31 seconds, 93.5°C. Dialed in at every opening (see [[Opening and Closing Checklist]]).

## Where it goes
- Café espresso and retail bags ($19.50 from 1 October, see [[Pricing Review]]).
- Almost every wholesale account: [[Seaview Hotel]], [[Driftwood Bakery]] and [[Northgate Co-op]].
- The default coffee in the [[Subscription Service]] "Harbor Dark only" plan.

## Ideas
The "Seaview Signature" custom blend would be a Harbor Dark variant, probably 50% Colombia, 30% Brazil and 20% Ethiopia for a brighter cup.
