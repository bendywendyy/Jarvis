# Subscription Service

Coffee subscriptions sold through our online store. Launched in 2021 and part of the online & subscriptions stream in the [[2026 Budget]] ($120k target).

## Plans
| Plan | Frequency | Price (current) | Share of subscribers |
|---|---|---|---|
| Harbor Dark only (12 oz) | Every 2 weeks | $20.00 + shipping | 46% |
| Roaster's Choice (12 oz single origin) | Every 2 weeks | $23.00 + shipping | 31% |
| Monthly duo (2 × 12 oz) | Monthly | $39.00 + shipping | 23% |

## Numbers (September 2026)
- **312 active subscribers**, down from 341 in March.
- **Churn is 6.8% per month**. The target is under 5%. The top cancellation reasons are "too much coffee" (38%), "price/shipping" (29%) and "moving/other" (33%).
- Average shipping cost to us has risen from $6.10 to $7.40 per parcel since January.

## Operations
- Roasted on Friday, packed Friday afternoon and shipped **Monday morning** (see [[Roasting Schedule]]).
- Mailer boxes from [[Bayside Packaging]] at $1.12 each.
- Beans for the Roaster's Choice plan come from the [[Single Origin Lineup]].

## Changes planned
1. **Pause instead of cancel**: add a "skip a delivery" and "pause for a month" button. This targets the "too much coffee" churn.
2. **Price change**: existing subscribers get +$1.00 per shipment from 1 January 2027, and new subscribers pay the new price now (see [[Pricing Review]]).
3. Free shipping on the monthly duo plan to push people toward larger, less frequent shipments.
4. Holiday gift subscriptions (3 or 6 months) promoted through the [[Instagram Strategy]] from mid-November.
5. Maybe link to the [[Loyalty Program]]: subscribers get a free café drink each month.
