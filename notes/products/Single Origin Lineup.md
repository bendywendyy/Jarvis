# Single Origin Lineup

We offer three single origins at any time. They're on filter in the café, sold in retail bags ($22.50 from 1 October) and in the "Roaster's Choice" [[Subscription Service]] plan. The lineup rotates roughly every quarter.

## Current lineup (autumn 2026)
| Coffee | Process | Notes | Cup score |
|---|---|---|---|
| Colombia, [[Finca La Esperanza]] | Washed | Red apple, panela, milk chocolate | 85.75 |
| Ethiopia, Guji (Hambela) | Natural | Blueberry, jasmine, cocoa nib | 87.0 |
| Guatemala, Huehuetenango | Washed | Caramel, orange, almond | 85.25 |

The Guatemala is also the base for the cold brew tonic on the [[Autumn Seasonal Menu]].

## Roasting
- Light to medium roasts in 10 kg batches with 13–14% roast loss. [[Daniel Reyes]] does all single-origin batches himself (see [[Roasting Schedule]]).
- Filter recipe: 22 g to 360 g water, 3:30 brew time.

## Performance
- Single origins are about 18% of retail bag sales, but they have a higher margin than [[Harbor Dark]] at the new prices.
- The Ethiopia natural sells out fastest. Consider buying 50% more of it next year.
